## Highest Priority

- Maxian, Mogilner, Strychalski (2020), "Computational estimates of mechanical constraints on cell migration through the extracellular matrix"
  - Why: closest conceptual match for active cortex, nucleus, ECM mechanics, cytoplasm/nucleoplasm flow, and migration-mode comparison.
  - Article: https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1008160
  - PDF: https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article/file?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1008160&type=printable
  - Code/data link from article: https://github.com/omaxian/CellMotility

- Gardel, Schneider, Aratyn-Schaus, Waterman (2010), "Mechanical Integration of Actin and Adhesion Dynamics in Cell Migration"
  - Why: practical review of protrusion, adhesion, contractility, and molecular clutch behavior.
  - Article: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4437624/
  - PubMed/PMC should expose a PDF button in browser.

- De Pascalis and Etienne-Manneville (2017), "Single and collective cell migration: the mechanics of adhesions"
  - Why: useful overview of adhesion mechanics and mechanosensing during migration.
  - Article: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5541834/
  - PubMed/PMC should expose a PDF button in browser.

## Core Background

- Joanny and Prost (2009), "Active gels as a description of the actin-myosin cytoskeleton"
  - Why: active gel framing for actomyosin/cortex mechanics.
  - Article: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2707794/

- Fedorchak, Kaminski, Lammerding (2014), "Cellular mechanosensing: getting to the nucleus of it all"
  - Why: nucleus as mechanically coupled structure and mechanosensor.
  - Article: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4252489/

- Mogilner and Manhart (2018), "Intracellular Fluid Mechanics: Coupling Cytoplasmic Flow with Active Cytoskeletal Gel"
  - Why: background for later cytoplasm/fluid upgrades.
  - Article: https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-fluid-010816-060238
  - DOI PDF link listed by Crossref/CiNii: https://www.annualreviews.org/doi/pdf/10.1146/annurev-fluid-010816-060238

- Charras and Sahai (2014), "Physical influences of the extracellular environment on cell migration"
  - Why: ECM stiffness, confinement, topology, and migration phenotype framing.
  - Article: https://www.nature.com/articles/nrm3897

- Pollard and Cooper (2009), "Actin, a Central Player in Cell Shape and Movement"
  - Why: compact biological background on actin as force-generating and structural machinery.
  - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1175862
  - Access may require institutional login.

- Mogilner and Oster (1996), "Cell motility driven by actin polymerization"
  - Why: classic actin polymerization/Brownian ratchet model for protrusion.
  - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/S0006-3495(96)79496-1
  - Author PDF found in search: https://cims.nyu.edu/~mogilner/actin.pdf
